ROOT CAUSE: BUG IN _REF_NEXT pattern
----------------------------------------
  parser.py defines:
    _REF_NEXT = re.compile(
        r'^(?:của|này|đó|trên|sau|dưới|và|hoặc|nêu|nói|quy|khoản|điểm|,|;)',
        re.UNICODE | re.IGNORECASE,
    )

  The token 'quy' was intended to catch cross-references like:
    '...Điều 5. Quy định chi tiết ...' (quy định = regulations)

  BUT with re.IGNORECASE, 'quy' is a PREFIX MATCH that also hits:
    'Quyền' (rights) — extremely common article-title word in Vietnamese law
    'Quy hoạch' (planning), 'Quy trình' (procedure), etc.

  Verification: _REF_NEXT.match('Quyền của doanh nghiệp...') = True

  Result: every article whose title begins with 'Quyền' is silently
  dropped by _find_article_boundaries() before the monotonic filter.
